# Eternal Dungeon: Lore

This file holds established world lore. Everything above **Flagged** is canon. Flagged items are **not canon** until Jeremy rules on them, so do not use them in content.

This file covers world facts only: peoples, creatures, places, figures, and powers. Classes, skills, items, and systems are defined in the Design Anchor. Where a lore entry names something that also exists as a mechanic, the Anchor wins on how it works.

When you add something that is likely to recur, add it here and flag it for review. See `tone-guide.md`.

## Cosmology

### The Highest Power (outside the game)
The Highest Power is the source of everything in existence, but it lies outside the game. Yermiyah serves the Highest Power. **Never use or mention it in content.** It is listed here only so its absence is deliberate.

### The pantheon
- **Yermiyah the Peasant** is the ultimate god within the world. He heard the call of greatness early in life. It began as visions and thoughts on how the world should work, and the call grew until he had to bring them into being himself. Through his genius the world's rules were made. He had no artistic ability, so he sought others to weave its surface. Yermiyah is not mentioned by name except in holy texts, but is mentioned by the titles "the peasant", "the ultimate", "the creator" or otherwise.
- **The pantheon can grow.** New gods may join it in the future, but only Jeremy adds them. Never invent a god of the pantheon.

### The old gods
Others helped Yermiyah bring the world into being. Those who are no longer active are known as the old gods.

**Usage:** Mention the old gods rarely, and always vaguely or cryptically, such as a half-remembered name, a worn shrine, or a saying whose origin nobody recalls. The details in this section are background for consistency. Never spell them out in content.
- **Yue the Weaver** devoted his life to creating lower realms. He shaped creatures and places: how they look, move, and interact. Yue disappeared from the realm.
- **Juliette the Savior** is a blessed soul from a faraway realm with infinite patience. She rewove everything that exists in her own manner, improving the works of Yue and Luca and finishing what they left undone. Without her, the world would be a mangled pile of grossness.
- **Luca the Dabbler** was born in the outer realms but has always lived within inner realms. He conceived and visualized much of the world's inner workings and its visual wonders, and he watched over its quality.
- **Stephan the Swift** is a young, kind, and humble soul. He is the world's bard, and his music accompanies those who explore it.

### The being of pure darkness
Acolytes serve an unnamed being of pure darkness. He is really nice once you get to know him. He is very specifically **not** the Shadow, and he makes a point of telling people so.

### The nature of the world
- The world is logical and orderly because it was created, deliberately, according to rules.
- Its purpose appears to be **success through conflict**: becoming the most powerful person in existence by working on oneself.
- The world was created with much evil in it. The purpose of evil is to be destroyed, and to help people grow by destroying it.
- **Evil keeps growing stronger,** even after a person stops growing. Eventually it becomes too strong to overcome. This is inevitable.

### Ascension
**Usage:** This is part of the sincere register. Write about it earnestly, never as a joke, and only in sincere moments or where requested (see `tone-guide.md`).

Death is not the end. The soul returns and rises a little higher each time, and this is called **Ascension**. There are several paths of Ascension:

- **The Phoenix:** The eternal cycle of rebirth. As sure as the arrival of death is the promise of life. The Phoenix attests to the ascension of purpose, independent of reincarnation.
- **The Archangel:** The peace of the heavens shines upon us from a guardian of light who brings both kindness and justice into being. The Archangels can be glimpsed as we ascend to the kingdom of Angels.
- **The Spirit:** A power within elevates and centers our being to pursue the eternal truth within a temporary dwelling. The Chakras of power are a wellspring of energy to balance our Ascension.
- **The Valkyrie:** Beyond our death is the echo of our glory, crying out to the future of humanity. Our accomplishments call attention to our lives and ascend us to greatness, as the Valkyrie rides on forever.
- **Gaia:** Our time is an instant in a sea of the infinite. So intertwined with the natural world are we that we unravel as we ascend. The forces of Gaia are the steadiness of the world and the womb of life that remains.
- **The Dragon:** This is the ancient, epic dragon spirit, distinct from mortal dragons (see Beasts). Our knowledge is insignificant compared to ancient wisdom. The dragon within ascends from our spirit, gifting truth to those who treasure the ancient ways and study them all their days.
- **The Blood:** A gallery of beasts was fashioned from our fear of the unknown. As we ascend in perspective, their mysteries are revealed and grant us power: knowledge of the mind of man, and of what the darkness holds over him.

### Realms
- **This world** is the realm where mortals live.
- **The demonic realm** exists parallel to this world (see Demons).
- **The Epic Realm** lies beyond the zones mortals usually reach. It holds Essences and is entered through portals, one of which was opened by Grimbolt Anvilsworth, the Master Smith (see Notable figures).
- **Parallel worlds** exist alongside this one, each with its own heroes. Heroes from other worlds can sometimes be encountered. (Behind the scenes, these are other players' games. In content, they are only ever parallel worlds.)
- **A mystical plane** lies beyond the veil of physical reality. It is a place of infinite wisdom, innocent and beautiful, and wizards harness and corrupt it to rain destruction on their enemies.

## Peoples and creatures

### Goblins
Goblins are a vicious race of man-eaters. They live in the woods, ambush weary travelers on the road, and boil them into stew. Small tribes waylay travelers, while large camps raid villages for sheep and babies.
- **There are three types of goblins.** Forest goblins are cunning and precise. Mountain goblins are hardy. Cave goblins are strong, and wield Darkness.
- **Fighters:** Most male goblins become fighters. They use crude weapons taken from their prey and rely on muscle and viciousness rather than skill.
- **Brutes:** The biggest goblins grow up to be bullies, masters of the traditional arts of wedgies and swirlies. The worst of them join the sacred order of **The Brute** and learn the ancient goblin martial art of **Bashum Gud**, which combines punching with boosting magic.
- **Shamans:** Goblins are deeply superstitious. For luck, they smear pigeon droppings on their foreheads and spin in circles before relieving themselves. Shamans are masters of these superstitions and enjoy inventing new ones for their clanmates to follow.
- **Goblin women** are so ugly that they took up alchemy, which they use to drug goblin men into marriage.
- **Chiefs:** A tribe's chief is chosen by girth. A fatter goblin loves eating more, so he will bring more food to the tribe. Shamans also teach that fatness indicates supernatural awesomeness, which makes for a good leader.
- **Pets and herbs:** Goblins keep **blobs** as pets. Blobs are small corrosive creatures that eat metal, clothing, and trinkets, and goblins feed them their victims' belongings. Blobs help on hunts by squirting themselves over travelers, and they entertain goblins by hopping about cutely and getting bashed into a satisfying mess. Goblins also season their stews with the **Pumpler Vine**, which tastes like a mix of deer droppings and baboon sweat. The vine is violent, and it eats as many goblins as goblins eat it.

### Beasts
The world is full of peaceful creatures who frolic in verdant forests. The beasts are not these creatures. Beasts exist only for violence and destruction. Beasts resist magic and are very strong.
- **Dragons** were once benevolent and at peace with the world. Then they discovered they were bigger and stronger than everything around them, and they have been destroying it ever since.
- **Whelps** are young dragons. They are named after what people say on meeting one: "Whelp, I guess we're done for." Their breath isn't fully developed yet. Whelp scales strongly resist their own element, a resistance that fades with age. Nobody knows why, though it is generally attributed to the world's need for balance.
- **Other beasts** include the **Uniceratops**, **Tigers**, **Wolves**, **Cat Men**, and **Goat Men**. None of them has established details yet.
- **The Mega Boar** is greater than the lion, which most people assume is king of the beasts. He has a violent temper, a sour disposition, and very few friends. This makes him more sour, and he takes it out on anyone he meets.

### Demons
A realm of demons exists parallel to this world. It contains only evil, cruel beings who love mischief and destruction. They occasionally come into this world, but mostly they stay home and are bad to each other. Visitors are treated to very bad hospitality. All demons are creatures of magic and can dispel it.
- **Imps** are tiny flying balls of teeth, claws, and magic. Their favorite targets are chair legs, support beams, and anything else whose collapse makes people fall on their behinds. Their second favorite target is so-called heroes.
- **Hellings** (pronounced Hell-Lings) live in the cluttered garbage districts where other demons dump their trash and half-eaten captives. There they have built tiny kingdoms with leaders, judges, and complicated legal codes.
- **Golems** cannot be called stupid, because stupidity requires a brain and golems don't have one. In their off hours they stand around staring into space with their single eye. In their on hours they do exactly the same thing.
- **Skeletons** are dead humans without the flesh. Having no thoughts or desires makes them perfect soldiers, but having no ears makes them very hard to command. They are already dead, so killing them again should be impossible. It isn't, for the sake of fairness.
- **Flamberts** are little black balls that are perpetually on fire. Any food they catch burns up before it reaches their mouths, so they are always hungry.

### The Shadow
**Usage:** The full story doesn't need to be revealed to players, but it isn't forbidden. Drop fragments of it here and there.

- **The Shadow King** was once a mortal wizard of great renown. He discovered a ritual of shadow and used its power to draw out the shadow essence of other living creatures. The shadow would then serve the Shadow King, while the mortal it came from remained listless and shallow. His henchmen sincerely believed in his unrivaled power and chose to become shadowed. His dominion spread over decades and covered a great portion of the world until a hero defeated him. **He is dead.**
- **The curse outlived him, and it spreads.** His defeat did not undo the corruption, and shadow beings can pull the shadows out of other creatures, so new shadow beings are still being born. It spreads through the world like a dark plague.
- **How the curse works:** The ritual, or the grasp of a shadow being, pulls a living creature's shadow out of it. The shadow becomes a corrupted, evil copy of its owner and roams the world on its own. People, animals, and monsters can all lose their shadows this way.
- **Shadowless creatures** are listless and not quite whole. A person standing in full sun with no shadow is a telltale sign.
- **Unshadowing** is defeating a corrupted shadow, which breaks the corruption and sends the shadow home to its owner. Returning shadows are embarrassed, follow their owners a little more closely afterward, and avoid eye contact.
- **Some shadows have no one to return to,** because their owners have died or they've been gone so long they've forgotten whose they were. Once defeated, these shadows dissipate.
- **The Shadow Beast** was chosen by the shadow beings as their new leader after the Shadow King's fall. It still lives and roams the world and is currently the most epic shadow being known.
- **The Shadow King and Shadow Queen statues** are relics of great power and store part of the essence of the Shadow King and his willing bride, the Shadow Queen.

## Notable figures

- **Twick the Hunter** was an old folk hero whose wisdom was entirely tautological. He is often quoted by rangers and other would-be heroes. "Slow warriors attack slowly. Fast warriors attack quickly." "Stronger hits kill better." "When you use your eyes you see more."
- **Bosun the Wise** was a sage. "A full vessel can contain little water, but an empty vessel can contain much."
- **Gambabuchu** (given name Steve) invented alchemy 100 years ago, which he called **Destroyination**. Later alchemists follow his techniques to the letter but ignore his naming schemes.
- **Branador the Bombadier** is, by his own account, the greatest alchemist in the known world and was a student of Gambabuchu. He discovered the secrets of the Mystic Forge and wrote a crafting guide about it. He is boastful and defensive, and he considers gems useless.
- **Grimbolt Anvilsworth, the Master Smith,** is a smith in town. His shadow was taken by the Shadow King's curse and fought heroes, and without it he couldn't smith properly. When it was unshadowed, it came home, and he reopened his shop. He can open a portal to the Epic Realm, so heroes can gather Essences there.

## Magic, alchemy, and craft

- **Magic is everywhere** in the world. Most people are bad at real magic, and mastering it takes countless hours of study and meditation.
- Wizards guard against magic by clearing their bodies' energy channels of unwanted gunk. This is very difficult to test.
- **Alchemy** is an innovation in this world and has achieved wonders. Its potions still taste terrible.
- **Gambabuchu's original potion names** were Rabadabadu (Health), Grubblesnuff (Mana), Afraganada (Buffing), Wowza (Alchemist Fire), Bahahahaha (Toxic Gas), and Holy Sh- (Holy Water). The buffing potion's side effects may include fever, nausea, strange rashes, high blood pressure, happy feet, twitching, ululation, and death. Some fanatic alchemists still use Gambabuchu's names, but the vast majority don't.
- **Scientists** exist as a profession in this world, and alchemists are counted among them.
- **The Mystic Forge** can be used to upgrade, combine, and transform items and is the anchor for the crafting system.
- **Essences** are potent ingredients found in the Epic Realm. They can pull power from one item and infuse it into another. Essences are the in-game ingredients used for advanced, endgame crafting recipes.

## Flagged (not canon until ruled)

- **Bog crocodiles** (Beasts; from Dungeon 0's Bog Crocodile event, 2026-10-09): they lurk in still swamp water and drift toward prey pretending to be logs, against the current, which logs don't do. Travelers post NO SWIMMING signs near the worst bogs, and the crocodiles eat the signs too.
- **Shrines of the old gods** (from Dungeon 0's Overgrown Shrine event, 2026-10-09): weathered altars whose names have worn past reading. Locals still leave offerings at them without remembering why. The Overgrown Shrine is Yue's: vines woven through it too neatly to be chance, the name worn to "a Y and a hopeful smudge", weaving imagery in its blessing (the world "stitched together", a knot in every muscle as its curse), and a hint of Juliette's work in the "bits nobody quite finished". Content never names Yue.
- **Goblin knots are terrible** (Goblins; from Dungeon 0's Goblin Tripwire and Goblin Camp, 2026-10-09): no self-respecting sailor would tie one, they come undone if you look at them sternly, and untying one still takes longer than tying it did. Goblin building in general runs on enthusiasm and optimism (the Caged Merchant's cage, the Sentry Tower).
- **Wolves are house-proud** (Beasts; from Dungeon 0's Wolf Den event, 2026-10-09): a den is kept tidy, with bones picked clean and sorted, and skulls lined up at the entrance the way other households keep flowerpots. They are still beasts and exist only for violence; they just like it neat.
