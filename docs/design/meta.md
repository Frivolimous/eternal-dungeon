# Meta progression

Progression has three layers: what lasts one run (Boons, Trinkets), what a hero keeps (levels, gear, Blessings), and what survives a reset (Artifacts, Gold unlocks).

## Progress

- Advance to harder dungeons. Difficulty is simply how deep you get.
- Every run grants Gold. Gold unlocks starting skill trees, harder dungeons and better dungeon loot.
- Collect heroes and gear.
- **? Economy:** not designed yet.

## Blessings

- One Blessing per hero, gained at a Temple.
- Its effect drops 5% for each death (each party wipe, tracked by the hero's death counter) and each repeat of a dungeon.
- It's removed by a party wipe, but kept when the party leaves a dungeon. Reincarnation fully restores Blessings and clears all decay.

## Prestige: Reincarnation

- Resets a hero in exchange for meta progress. It fully restores Blessings (clearing all decay), improves Artifacts and changes the hero's race. Artifacts are the endgame grind.
- Artifacts unlock in a series when you beat a dungeon's last level. They're shared equippables (up to 3 per hero) and level up to 10.
