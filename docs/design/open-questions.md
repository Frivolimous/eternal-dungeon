# Open questions

Eleven items are still open. Untested design ideas are listed under Ideas still open in [Meta](meta.md).

- [ ] **? Economy:** not designed yet. Gold income per run, unlock prices, shop and crafting costs. Left open on purpose.
- [ ] **Crafted items:** what minimum dungeon do they get? Probably the crafting hero's dungeon level, like the shop.
- [ ] **Weight penalty:** tune the placeholder (−25% Hit and −10 Speed per tier short).
- [ ] **Map sizes:** how many Nodes a small, medium and big Map has, and how much Camp recovery restores.
- [ ] **Flee:** how fleeing combat works: does it always succeed, is it a chance, does it cost AP, and from which tiles? Its results are set (see Exploration › Combat in exploration).
- [ ] **Exhaustion numbers:** the penalty is a Speed reduction (Exhausted, then Severe) plus worse Event rolls. How big? Idea to explore: tie it to the weight penalty from gear.
- [ ] **Blessings, Boons, Trinkets and Temples:** not designed yet. They also need adding to the reward mechanisms and to the Interactables (Temples may be the Shrine Interactable).
- [ ] **Extended Maps:** how endless play after completing a dungeon fits the Map model.
- [ ] **Overland and towns:** not designed yet, including whether there is more than one town.
- [ ] **Collapse and units in the other area:** when an area's front row empties, should the forward collapse also move units from the other side standing in it (a Rogue that moved in)? Today it does. Suggested answer: no, collapse moves only the area's own units. Code unchanged until decided.
- [ ] ***Tenacity:** the intent of "Control Duration −1" is unclear. Parked until combat tuning.
