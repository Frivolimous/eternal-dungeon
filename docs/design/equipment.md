# Equipment & character power

Equipment provides the largest share of a hero's power (37%), followed by class (28%) and Artifacts (25%), which are the endgame grind that keeps the game going long-term. These shares are placeholders until tuning. Base stats and race together provide only 6%.

| Source | Share of power |
| --- | --- |
| Equipment | 37% |
| Artifacts | 25% |
| Class | 28% |
| Base stats | 4% |
| Blessing | 4% |
| Race | 2% |

## Slots

- **Primary equipment:** Weapon, Off-hand, Armor, Helmet. A two-handed weapon fills the Off-hand slot. Any weapon can go in the Off-hand, with its own weight penalty plus the dual-wield penalties.
- **Accessory:** 1 slot for an enchanted ring, book or similar.
- **Spells:** 0–3 slots (1 per Spell+ class).
- **Belt slots:** 1–4 (1 base, plus 1 per Belt+ class). Belt items are consumables with limited uses per dungeon: they're used up across the whole expedition and don't refill between fights. Camps, Sanctuaries, Events and exploration skills (such as the Tinkerer's) can restore charges. Talismans are the exception: they give a small passive stat bonus and are never used up.
- **Party pack:** spare consumables the party carries in a dungeon, beyond what fits in belts. Moving them into a hero's belt happens through the Hero Panel, outside combat (decided 2026-10-08).
- **Artifacts:** up to 3, equipped on Reincarnation.

Each class a hero takes (Primary, Secondary and Dabble alike) adds one point to its family:

| Family | Each class adds |
| --- | --- |
| Primary+ | Use of higher-tier primary equipment without a penalty |
| Belt+ | 1 more Belt slot |
| Spell+ | 1 more Spell slot |

## Rules

- **Sources:** dungeon drops, the Town shop, crafting and rewards.
- **Item level (1–15):** items are found at levels up to 15, or upgraded in Town at a very high cost.
- **Rarity:** Normal (a basic item), Enchanted (a random prefix), Unique (a preset item with a fixed stat combo).
- **Suffixes:** any rarity can also carry suffixes that add power.
- **Restrictions:** no level or race restrictions. Every piece of primary equipment has a gear tier (0–2) set by its type. A hero can equip gear above their Primary+ class count, but takes a weight penalty for it, for example −50% Hit with a greatsword. Placeholder until tuning: −25% Hit and −10 Speed for each tier the hero is short. Gear tier (by type) is separate from item level (1–15, by power).
- **Passing gear:** every item is stamped with a minimum dungeon, the dungeon it came from. Only heroes who have reached that dungeon can hold or equip it, so gear moves freely sideways and forward but never back. For example, Dungeon 3 gear can't go to a hero in Dungeon 2. This stops players power-leveling new heroes with high-level gear.
- **Stash:** one shared stash for the whole roster, used in Town. Each item's minimum-dungeon stamp decides which heroes can take it. May be refined later.
- **Shop:** the shop is browsed as a specific hero, and it stocks gear stamped with that hero's dungeon level.
